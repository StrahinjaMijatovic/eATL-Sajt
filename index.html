Skip the flight

One flight. One record. Preflight, Flight, Flight Data and Defects, signed by the people responsible for each part, then closed by Technical Staff and archived as a PDF for CAMO. Nothing retyped, nothing lost in the seat pocket.

From snag to release to service.

  1. ReportedThe Commander logs the defect on the record, by ATA chapter, with photos. LINE, CAMO and OPS are notified.
  2. DeferredAgainst an item from the operator's MEL/CDL library. eATL calculates the deadline and adds it to the Deferred Defect List (DDL).
  3. Record closedCommander and Technical Staff sign. The record locks and goes to CAMO; the defect stays on the DDL and the next flight isn't blocked.
  4. RectifiedMaintenance staff only: action taken, Part-66 number, signature and company stamp in the CRS block.

Right aircraft. Proven, not assumed.

Before a new record opens, the commander scans the QR sticker in the cockpit. The code is signed with the operator's ES256 key and verified on the device, offline. Similar registrations stop being a trap.

Signed means sealed.

Each signature stores a snapshot of the signer's own drawn signature, the time, and a hash of the record content, linked to the previous one. Change a signed value and the chain shows it; the only way to correct it is an Amendment linked to the original, which stays visible.

    The rules paper never enforced.

    • Missing a mandatory field?The section cannot be signed. The screen names every missing field and how to resolve it. Same validator offline and on the server.
    • Open defect, not deferred?Preflight cannot be signed until it is rectified by maintenance or deferred against a library MEL/CDL item.
    • MEL deadline expired?The ATL is hard-blocked for everyone except maintenance staff. No "continue anyway".
    • First flight of the UTC day?Tyre pressures are required. Later flights that day carry them over, marked as carried.

    Deadlines the crew doesn't have to count.

    Defer against the operator's own MEL/CDL library and eATL calculates the deadline by category. The day of discovery doesn't count. The Deferred Defect List updates itself.
