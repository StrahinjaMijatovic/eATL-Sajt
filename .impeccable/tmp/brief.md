# Surface brief: eATL marketing site (index.html)

Scope: single-page marketing site for eATL. Mode: Persuade. Audience: operators (accountable managers, CAMO, technical/flight-ops directors). Action: contact e-mail (address placeholder until confirmed). Proof: real product mechanisms from PRODUCT.md, demonstrated with synthetic, labelled demo data; app screenshot gallery with replaceable slots. Constraints: English; no invented customers, certifications, approvals, pricing. User chose the category standard (canon) after two rounds; quality bar: Apple iPad Pro page, Linear/Stripe, Boom/Joby, ForeFlight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard (deep-navy aviation SaaS, iPad, scroll-told product story) executed at Apple-page fidelity; refuses the static hero + feature-card grid by telling the story as pinned, scroll-scrubbed scenes where the aircraft literally becomes its record.

OWN-WORLD: Night apron navy ground (#06111F family), brand blue from the eATL mark, white paper sheet, taxi-line yellow only for ground markings; status green/amber/red only semantic. Archivo (expanded, heavy) display, Geist body, JetBrains Mono for registrations, times and hashes. iPad frames in graphite, hairline rules, AUTO/PREV/CARRIED markers in blue.

STORY: Visitor sees a parked jet turn into a filled ATL record, understands the lifecycle Preflight to Close, believes it through QR confirmation, hash-chained signatures, blocking validation and offline sync, sees real screens, then e-mails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top nav (eATL mark left, 4 links, Contact button right). Plan-view business jet parked on an apron with yellow lead-in line, filling ~60% width centre-right; headline "The tech log, without the paper." left at display scale, one-line sub, primary Contact button + secondary "See how it works". On scroll the hero pins: leader lines run from tyres, engines, tanks to an ATL sheet that slides in and fills field by field.
